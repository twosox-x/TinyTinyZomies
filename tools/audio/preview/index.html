Notes for review

Adaptive music: press Play music, then run the squad firefight scene. The Last Stand should come in after about 30–45 s of sustained fighting and give way to The Infection about 40 s after the scene stops. Horde: zoomed out it is now quieter and muffled. Not yet found as CC0: a recorded "Ammo!" callout (stand-ins: "Reloading!", "Call for backup!"). The UI set was expanded after review: 25 tactile switch / latch sounds, including a chirp per squad class. Loudness: each sound is levelled to a per-category target (gunfire −13, explosions −11, chain −9, voices −15, zombies −19, footsteps −24, ambience loops −26 LUFS-ish); the page has a master limiter.
All source recordings: CC0 1.0 from OpenGameArt.org; full per-file list in assets/audio/sources.json and docs/SOUND_SOURCES.md on branch sessionD/sound. Processing: tools/audio/recipes.py.
